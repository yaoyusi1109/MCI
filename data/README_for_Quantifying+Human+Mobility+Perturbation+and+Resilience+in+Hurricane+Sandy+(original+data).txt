Data was collected by Qi Wang and John E. Taylor using the open API provided by Twitter.

The .xlsx file ("Geographical Coordinates Data From Active Users (Calculate Center of Mass and Radius of Gyration).xlsx") provides the geographical coordinates from 2240 active users. We removed their identities from our dataset. Also, to protect the privacy of these Twitter users, the longitudes of the geographical coordinates were modified so the locations are shown in an area close to Beijing, China instead of New York City where the data were actually collected. We conducted additional analyses and confirmed that the results calculated by the Haversine formula based on the modified data had no difference from the ones based on the original data.

The .txt files provide the lengths of displacements during and after the attack of Hurricane Sandy (4pm October 29, 2012 to 4pm November 10). Each file (Displacement Data from Day XX.txt) represents data collected each 24-hour period.
