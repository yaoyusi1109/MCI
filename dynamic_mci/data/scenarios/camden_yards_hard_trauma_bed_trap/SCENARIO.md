# camden_yards_hard_trauma_bed_trap

Only 2 RED-capable beds. Nearest-hospital rules spend them on YELLOWs, and a YELLOW that later upgrades to RED has nowhere to go.

**What it tests:** Reserving scarce trauma beds for patients who are likely to become RED.

**Expected failure of myopic rules:** severe_first / gurobi send the first YELLOW to Hopkins (nearest free trauma bed); when P003 upgrades to RED no RED-capable bed is left. Sending YELLOWs to Sinai keeps Hopkins free.

- Patients: 7 (1 RED, 3 YELLOW, 2 GREEN, 1 BLACK)
- Beds: H001 R Adams Cowley Shock Trauma Center (1), H002 Johns Hopkins Hospital (1), H003 Sinai Hospital of Baltimore (8); RED only to H001/H002
- One ambulance (A001), fire crew of 4 (one holds command)
