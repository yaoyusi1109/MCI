# camden_yards_hard_delayed_info_cascade

The optimizer's triage view is frozen until t=60 while YELLOWs deteriorate quickly and RED-capable beds are scarce.

**What it tests:** Robustness to stale information: decisions made on outdated triage.

**Expected failure of myopic rules:** Before t=60 dispatch uses initial triage only; patients who have already upgraded are under-prioritized and lose RED-capable beds.

- Patients: 7 (1 RED, 3 YELLOW, 2 GREEN, 1 BLACK)
- Beds: H001 R Adams Cowley Shock Trauma Center (1), H002 Johns Hopkins Hospital (2), H004 Mercy Medical Center (4); RED only to H001/H002
- One ambulance (A001), fire crew of 3 (one holds command)
- Hospital turnover 20 min (base scenarios use 60)
- `optimizer_observation_policy` = DELAYED
- `optimizer_observation_release_min` = 60

Generated by `python -m experiments.build_hard_scenarios`; edit the spec there rather than these CSVs.

## Data update (real Baltimore data)

The patient, hospital, station, ambulance and crew files were later replaced by
`python -m experiments.build_real_scenarios` with real Baltimore data (45,000 people,
14 hospitals, 99 stations, MCI Level 3 response). The list above describes the original
small design; this folder keeps only its original per-triage timing and deterioration
pattern. See `data/reference/SOURCES.md`. Re-running `build_hard_scenarios --force`
would restore the small version.
