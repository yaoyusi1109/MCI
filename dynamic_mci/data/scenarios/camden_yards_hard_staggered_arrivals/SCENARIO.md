# camden_yards_hard_staggered_arrivals

Walking wounded are ready at t=0, but two REDs are extricated at t=45 and t=75. Greedy rules use the trauma beds before the REDs appear.

**What it tests:** Holding capacity for patients who are not yet visible (look-ahead).

**Expected failure of myopic rules:** The first YELLOWs take Shock Trauma and Hopkins (nearest free beds); the late REDs then find too few RED-capable beds. Sending early YELLOWs to Sinai (far, 6 beds) would keep them free.

- Patients: 8 (2 RED, 4 YELLOW, 2 GREEN)
- Beds: H001 R Adams Cowley Shock Trauma Center (1), H002 Johns Hopkins Hospital (2), H003 Sinai Hospital of Baltimore (6); RED only to H001/H002
- One ambulance (A001), fire crew of 4 (one holds command)
- Hospital turnover 20 min (base scenarios use 60)

Generated by `python -m experiments.build_hard_scenarios`; edit the spec there rather than these CSVs.

## Data update (real Baltimore data)

The patient, hospital, station, ambulance and crew files were later replaced by
`python -m experiments.build_real_scenarios` with real Baltimore data (45,000 people,
14 hospitals, 99 stations, MCI Level 3 response). The list above describes the original
small design; this folder keeps only its original per-triage timing and deterioration
pattern. See `data/reference/SOURCES.md`. Re-running `build_hard_scenarios --force`
would restore the small version.
