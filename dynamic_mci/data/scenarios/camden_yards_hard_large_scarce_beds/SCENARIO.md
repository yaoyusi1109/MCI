# camden_yards_hard_large_scarce_beds

18 patients (4 RED, 7 YELLOW, 5 GREEN, 2 BLACK), 7 hospitals with 19 beds in total, one ambulance. The main stress test.

**What it tests:** Everything at once: bed reservation, deterioration, round-trip cost, and a large backlog the horizon cannot clear.

**Expected failure of myopic rules:** Large differences in deliveries, BLACK transitions and expected survivors between policies.

- Patients: 18 (4 RED, 7 YELLOW, 5 GREEN, 2 BLACK)
- Beds: H001 R Adams Cowley Shock Trauma Center (2), H002 Johns Hopkins Hospital (2), H003 Sinai Hospital of Baltimore (4), H004 Mercy Medical Center (3), H005 MedStar Harbor Hospital (3), H006 Johns Hopkins Bayview Medical Center (3), H007 MedStar Union Memorial Hospital (2); RED only to H001/H002
- One ambulance (A001), fire crew of 6 (one holds command)
- Hospital turnover 20 min (base scenarios use 60)

Generated by `python -m experiments.build_hard_scenarios`; edit the spec there rather than these CSVs.

## Data update (real Baltimore data)

The patient, hospital, station, ambulance and crew files were later replaced by
`python -m experiments.build_real_scenarios` with real Baltimore data (45,000 people,
14 hospitals, 99 stations, MCI Level 3 response). The list above describes the original
small design; this folder keeps only its original per-triage timing and deterioration
pattern. See `data/reference/SOURCES.md`. Re-running `build_hard_scenarios --force`
would restore the small version.
