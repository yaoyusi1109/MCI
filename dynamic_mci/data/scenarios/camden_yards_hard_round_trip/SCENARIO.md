# camden_yards_hard_round_trip

Near hospitals have 2 beds each; far hospitals (Sinai, Bayview) have many. With one ambulance and a 240-min horizon, every far trip costs a whole extra cycle.

**What it tests:** Trading cycle time against bed scarcity: who should use the near beds and when to accept a long round trip.

**Expected failure of myopic rules:** Not every patient can be delivered within 240 min. Load-balancing sends patients far and loses trips; nearest-first fills near beds with whoever is first.

- Patients: 12 (3 RED, 5 YELLOW, 4 GREEN)
- Beds: H001 R Adams Cowley Shock Trauma Center (2), H002 Johns Hopkins Hospital (2), H004 Mercy Medical Center (2), H003 Sinai Hospital of Baltimore (10), H006 Johns Hopkins Bayview Medical Center (8); RED only to H001/H002
- One ambulance (A001), fire crew of 4 (one holds command)
- Hospital turnover 20 min (base scenarios use 60)

Generated by `python -m experiments.build_hard_scenarios`; edit the spec there rather than these CSVs.

## Data update (real Baltimore data)

The patient, hospital, station, ambulance and crew files were later replaced by

small design; this folder keeps only its original per-triage timing and deterioration
