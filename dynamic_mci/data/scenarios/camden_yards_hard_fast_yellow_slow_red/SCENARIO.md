# camden_yards_hard_fast_yellow_slow_red

Two stable REDs and three fast-deteriorating YELLOWs with only one free stabilizer. Taking REDs first lets YELLOWs slide to RED, then BLACK.

**What it tests:** Value of knowing deterioration speed, not just the current triage colour. All current policies see only triage colour, so all are expected to do poorly here; the gap shows what a deterioration-aware model could gain.

**Expected failure of myopic rules:** Every triage-only policy serves P001/P002 first; P003/P004/P006 upgrade and some reach BLACK before the ambulance returns.

- Patients: 6 (2 RED, 3 YELLOW, 1 GREEN)
- Beds: H001 R Adams Cowley Shock Trauma Center (3), H002 Johns Hopkins Hospital (3), H004 Mercy Medical Center (6); RED only to H001/H002
- One ambulance (A001), fire crew of 2 (one holds command)
- Hospital turnover 20 min (base scenarios use 60)
